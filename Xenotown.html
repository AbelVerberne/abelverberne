Graduation project

Xenotown is a story of someone who's new to town, with no connection finds himself upon the subject of his common curiousity. The character however does not find the alien to be how he expected it to be, though may find their plight for community to be not too dissimilar.
This is part one of a four part story, in which the alien and our character aid eachother in regaining community.

Being the last project of my academic carreer at Minerva, I wanted to put as many of the competencies I had learned into Xenotown. This drove me to choose a quality driven approach, deciding to apply many different methods and mediums over a unified one. This strategy drove down the runtime of the animation but resulted in a more visually striking end product.
Methods used in this project: digital 2D animation (vector & bitmap), Blender 3D, ink, watercolor, plotter driven illustration, all in various combinations.
